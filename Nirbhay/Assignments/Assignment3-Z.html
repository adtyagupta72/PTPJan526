TAJ MAHAL : THE CROWN OF INDIA

location

The Taj Mahal is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. You can reach Agra by train, bus, or car from Delhi. The nearest railway station is Agra Cantt.

History

Built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal,
construction started in 1632 and took around 20 years to complete.
